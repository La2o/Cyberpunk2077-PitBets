## Summary (short description)

Bet eddies on underground NPC fights in Pacifica and Kabuki. Each fighter's cyberware sets their odds. Sabotage a fighter with quickhacks, but if the bookies catch you, they come for you.

## Description (BBCode)

```
[size=5][b]Pit Bets[/b][/size]

Night City has a fight scene, and somebody is always taking bets.

[b]Features[/b]
[list]
[*]Two venues: [b]Pacifica[/b] (Animals' book, 30% base detection) and [b]Kabuki[/b] (Tyger Claws' book, 20%)
[*]Boxers, chromed brawlers and (experimental) mechs, each rolled with random cyberware
[*]Cyberware sets the odds [i]and[/i] buffs the fighter's real health and armor
[*]Draft any NPC you're looking at into a corner
[*]Sabotage with RAM: Short Circuit, Cyberware Malfunction, Armor Shred. Shooting or hacking a fighter yourself counts too
[*]Get caught and your bet is void, and the bookie's muscle comes for you
[*]Live HUD with health bars, a judges' decision on timeout, saved lifetime stats
[/list]

[b]How to use[/b]
Stand somewhere open, open the CET overlay, choose a venue, press [i]Get tonight's card[/i], then [i]Open the book[/i]. Bet before the bell.

[b]Requirements[/b]
[list]
[*]Cyber Engine Tweaks
[*]Codeware
[/list]

[b]Install[/b]
Vortex / MO2, or extract into the game folder.

[b]Customise[/b]
Edit [i]roster.lua[/i] to add fighter records, cyberware and fighter names. The in-game [i]Record check[/i] panel shows which records loaded.

[b]Known limits[/b]
Fights happen where you stand. The CET overlay does not pause the game, so the bookies will not wait for you. Nearby gang members may join in.

Tested on 2.5 with CET and Codeware 1.21.

Source: https://github.com/La2o/PitBets
```
